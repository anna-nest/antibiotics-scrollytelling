# Antibiotic Resistance Scrollytelling

An interactive visualization exploring the history of antibiotic discovery and antimicrobial resistance.

## Technologies
- JavaScript (GSAP, D3.js, Scrollama)
- HTML/CSS/SVG
- Data visualization

## View Live
[Live Project](https://anna-nest.github.io/antibiotics-scrollytelling/)

## Development
Built with AI assistance (ChatGPT/Claude) for rapid prototyping and animation logic.

## Credits
Data sources: 
•	Historical mortality data — National Center for Health Statistics, CDC Archive (“Leading Causes of Death, 1900–1932 and U.S. data thereafter”).
•	Recent global mortality data — Institute for Health Metrics and Evaluation (IHME), Global Burden of Disease Study 2024.
•	U.S. antibiotic / drug approvals — compiled by Our World in Data (from FDA / CDER records).
•	Antimicrobial resistance forecasts — The Lancet (2024), Global burden of bacterial antimicrobial resistance 1990–2021, with forecasts to 2050.
•	Population & death projections — United Nations, World Population Prospects 2024.
